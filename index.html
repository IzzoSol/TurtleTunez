👋

          
Welcome to TurtleTunez

          
A gesture-controlled music studio. Your webcam is the controller — one hand shapes harmony, the other sculpts expression.

        

        

          
✋

          
Left hand = harmony

          
Fingers pick the chord degree (I–VII), tilt picks major/minor. Try holding 1 finger up, then 2, 3…

        

        

          
🤚

          
Right hand = expression

          
Wrist height = volume, fingers = voicing, thumb = octave up, tilt = filter sweep.

        

        

          
🎛

          
Pick a mode

          
Solo, Duo, Band (agents join you), Beats (drum machine focus), Sample (import a song), Cymatics (visuals), AI (auto-composes), Hum & Riff (hum or play guitar and it performs your melody). Pop out the right drawer for sound, beats, agents, and the guide.
